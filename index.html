Why two calorie levels
Training vs rest day split
More calories on training days fuels performance and recovery. Fewer on rest days deepens the deficit when the body needs fuel least. Weekly average stays at ~1,519 kcal.
Weekly calorie maths
10,645 kcal/week
4 training days × 1,570 + 3 rest days × 1,455 = 10,645. vs TDEE of 14,700 = deficit of ~4,055 kcal/week = ~0.5 kg fat loss/week.

Protein is the priority. Hit 120g every day — training or rest. If you miss calories, miss them from carbs or fats, not protein. Protein is what keeps your muscle intact while the fat comes off.
Cooking oil is the most common hidden calorie. Max 2–3 tsp total oil across all meals per day. Use a teaspoon to measure — do not eyeball it.
